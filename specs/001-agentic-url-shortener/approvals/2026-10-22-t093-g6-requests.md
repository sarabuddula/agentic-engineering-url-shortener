# Human Decision 2026-10-22: G6 accepted-limitation requests for T093 (C-005, additive contract and schema)

Date: 2026-10-22
Approver: Shilpa (human candidate)
Recorded words: "ok", given in reply to a written proposal (prepared by the AI assistant, which does not approve).

## Problem found
G6 (ACCEPTED_LIMITATION) could not be used in production: no endpoint requested a G6 approval, and approving one only
appended the text to the approval reason - no ACCEPTED_LIMITATION row was ever written (only a test inserted one). T093
("ACCEPTED_LIMITATION rows with approval references") could not be met.

## Decision
1. Additive endpoint `POST /api/v1/orchestrator/runs/{runId}/accepted-limitations` (ENGINEER), body
   `{ "limitation_id": "L-SC-01", "description": "...", "compensating_control": "..." }`: records the request in a new
   table LIMITATION_REQUEST and opens a G6 approval for a RELEASE_OWNER other than the requester; 201
   `{ approval_id, limitation_id, status: PENDING_APPROVAL }`; 400 for missing fields or a malformed ID; 409
   LIMITATION_CONFLICT for a terminal run.
2. Approving the G6 approval through the approvals endpoint (with `accepted_limitation_text`, the human's own words)
   writes the ACCEPTED_LIMITATION row (description, compensating control, approval reference, approved_at), audited and
   recorded in the decision lineage. A rejection writes no row.
3. A fresh REPLAY run is used: one G6 per open limitation (L-SC-01, 02, 03, 04, 06, 07; L-SC-05 is withdrawn), each
   decided individually by the candidate, who also determines whether L-SC-03 affects a non-waivable principle
   (spec Amendment Appendix C). With every mandatory check passing, N12 then recommends READY_WITH_ACCEPTED_LIMITATIONS.
